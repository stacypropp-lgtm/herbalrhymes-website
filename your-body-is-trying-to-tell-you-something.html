Your doctor says you’re fine. You don’t feel fine.

You wake up at 3am for no reason. You’re bloated after every meal. Your energy crashes every afternoon. Your headaches always hit the same spot. And every visit ends the same way: “Your labs look normal. It’s probably stress.”

Here’s the truth: your body isn’t broken. It’s talking to you. Every symptom — the insomnia, the fatigue, the brain fog, the PMS, the skin flare-ups — is a message. Once you learn to decode those messages, everything changes.

In this guide, Dr. Stacy Peck translates 2,000 years of Chinese medicine into language anyone can understand — no medical degree required. It reads like a smart friend who happens to be a doctor explaining what’s going on over coffee: warm, funny, and backed by both ancient wisdom and modern research.

Questions readers ask

Do I need any background in Chinese medicine to read it?

No. The book is written for the general reader. Every concept is explained in everyday language, and each symptom entry stands on its own, so you can skip straight to what’s bothering you.

Is it available on Kindle Unlimited?

Yes — the Kindle edition is included in Kindle Unlimited at no extra cost, or $2.99 to buy. The paperback is $18.99.

How is it organized?

By symptom. Look up what you’re experiencing — 3am waking, bloating, headaches, fatigue, PMS, brain fog and more — and find the Chinese medicine explanation, acupressure points, and food therapy for it.

Does it replace medical care?

No. It helps you understand what your body may be signaling and gives you safe self-care tools. Keep seeing your doctor for diagnosis and treatment.
