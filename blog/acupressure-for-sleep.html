Acupressure for Sleep: 5 Points That Actually Help

Can’t fall asleep — or keep waking at 3am? Five acupressure points from a licensed acupuncturist, free to try tonight.

By Dr. Stacy Peck, DACM, L.Ac.

If you’re reading this at 2am, you’re not alone — and you don’t need another app, another supplement, or another lecture about screen time. In my clinic, the people who sleep worst share two patterns: they can’t fall asleep because their mind won’t stop, or they fall asleep fine and then wake at 3am like someone flipped a switch.

Chinese medicine has used pressure points for both problems for a very long time. Here are the five I teach my own patients — free, tonight, with nothing but your hands.

Why 3am waking is a Liver problem

In Chinese medicine, the body’s energy moves through the organ systems on a daily clock. 1–3am is Liver time. If you consistently wake in that window — especially with frustration, vivid dreams, or a mind that immediately starts chewing on tomorrow — that’s the classic picture of Liver energy stuck. The fix isn’t sedation. It’s moving what’s stuck.

The 5 points (short version)

  1. Shen Men (ear) — in the triangular hollow near the top inside of your ear. The great calmer of Chinese medicine; press both ears 1–2 minutes.
  2. An Mian (“Peaceful Sleep”) — in the soft hollow right behind your earlobe, below the bony bump. A point that exists specifically for sleep. Press both sides for 2 minutes.
  3. Yin Tang — between your eyebrows. Releases the forehead tension of a day spent thinking. 1–2 minutes.
  4. San Yin Jiao (Spleen 6) — four finger-widths above your inner ankle bone, just behind the shin bone. Calms the whole system. (Skip this one during pregnancy.)
  5. Yong Quan (Kidney 1) — on the sole, in the hollow behind the ball of your foot, about a third of the way from toes to heel. Draws racing energy downward. Press 2 minutes per foot, or roll a tennis ball under your foot.

The 3am rescue

Wake at 1–3am and can’t drift off? Add Tai Chong (Liver 3): find the web between your big toe and second toe, slide back about two finger-widths staying in the groove in line with the web, and press the tender dip firmly for 2 minutes. Don’t check your phone. Don’t turn on the light. Just press, breathe, and let yourself drift.
