Questions readers ask

How is this different from Your Body Is Trying to Tell You Something?

Your Body teaches you to read symptoms as messages. What Would Your Acupuncturist Say? is the practical A-to-Z reference: look up a concern, get the routine and the recipe. They work well together, and each stands alone.

Is it available on Kindle Unlimited?

Yes — the Kindle edition is included in Kindle Unlimited at no extra cost, or $6.99 to buy. The paperback is $18.99.

Do I need special tools for the routines?

No. The 15 acupressure routines use only your hands. Optional sections on ear seeds, gua sha, and cupping explain the inexpensive tools involved.

Does it replace seeing an acupuncturist?

No. It helps you get more out of your care and gives you safe things to do at home between visits.
